Research

Quantifying biology that is too small, too fast, or too noisy to see directly

My work sits between mathematics and cell biology. Microscopy produces enormous, sparse and often ambiguous data; the interesting question is usually not what an image looks like, but what can be measured from it reliably. I build methods — topological, statistical and increasingly generative — that make those measurements possible, and then apply them to membrane organisation, cell signalling and immunotherapy.
